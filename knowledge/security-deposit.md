# Security deposit

## deposit-1

The security deposit is the `securityDeposit` value on the vehicle. It is payable at pickup and is not included in `totalPrice`.

## deposit-2

The deposit is refundable after the vehicle is returned with the agreed fuel level and no damage beyond normal wear.

## deposit-3

The deposit may be withheld in whole or in part for damage, missing fuel, or unpaid late fees.
