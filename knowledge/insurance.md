# Insurance

## ins-1

Every listed vehicle includes basic third-party liability cover for the rental period.

## ins-2

Damage to the rented vehicle above normal wear is the renter’s responsibility up to the security deposit. A deductible of ₹2,000 applies before the deposit is used.

## ins-3

The prototype does not sell add-on cover. Questions about add-on policies are outside the knowledge base.
