# Fuel policy

## fuel-1

Vehicles are handed over with a full tank. The renter returns the vehicle with a full tank of the same fuel type.

## fuel-2

If the tank is short, the shortfall is charged at the station rate plus a ₹500 service fee. This prototype explains the rule and does not invoice it.

## fuel-3

The renter uses the correct fuel for the vehicle. Wrong fuel is treated as damage under the insurance and deposit policies.
